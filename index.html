About

Anna Borodina

I am a Holistic Nutrition Clinical Practitioner with 12+ years of medical experience in the surgical department of the Kharkiv Ophthalmological Hospital named after Prof. L. L. Girshman.

In Canada, I graduated from the Canadian School of Natural Nutrition, Calgary Branch, and I am a member of the CSNN Alumni Association and the Canadian Association of Natural Nutritional Practitioners.

My approach combines clinical thinking, nutrition, symptom review, lifestyle strategy and realistic steps that a person can implement without overwhelm.

What you can come with

Nutrition, habits and body-support work

We review nutrition, lifestyle and habits that may affect your wellbeing, then choose practical steps for your situation.

Digestive concerns

bloating, gas, diarrhea/constipation, heartburn and digestive discomfort

Painful periods

nutrition and lifestyle review around factors that may influence cycle wellbeing

Excess weight

meal structure, eating habits, satiety, routine and sustainable change

Body support

frequent cold sores, styes, respiratory infections, post-COVID state, physical training, pregnancy and lactation within nutrition support scope

Thyroid support

nutrition, nutrients, routine, stress and collaboration with a physician when needed

Low energy

difficulty getting up, mood swings, reduced focus, overeating, cravings, frequent snacking
